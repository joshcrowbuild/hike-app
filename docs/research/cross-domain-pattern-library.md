## 2. Thesis — "cockpit-grade discipline to the trailhead"

The product thesis survives the pressure-test, but the deepest finding sharpens it: **the discipline is not aviation's — it is a shared architecture that thirteen independent high-consequence disciplines converged on without coordinating.** That convergence means Adventure Planner's six invariants are not design preferences; they are discovered law. Thirteen disciplines built the same four load-bearing moves:

1. **Two orthogonal axes, never collapsed.** Desirability/recommendation is held strictly separate from confidence/certainty. GRADE splits recommendation-strength from evidence-certainty; SAR computes POS = POC × POD (where-it-is × how-well-we-looked); the Admiralty code rates source-reliability (letter) independently of information-credibility (numeral); marine CATZOC turns low confidence into a wider *safety margin*, never a chart demotion. **This is invariant 2 as discovered law.**

2. **The tool scores, the human decides.** Every domain ships one auditable advisory verdict naming a single binding constraint, and every domain forbids the verdict from being an oracle. The FAA is explicit: *"the FRAT is not designed to make the go/no-go decision for you."* Avalanche doctrine separates hazard (nature) from risk (your exposure).

3. **Safety is a perishable state, re-established continuously.** Marine APEM, wildland LCES, and aviation dispatch monitor-and-recall all treat safety not as a one-time pre-trip check but as a live-monitored state with pre-set trigger points and a forward-framed recall.

4. **Corroboration is the engine, but independence is the governor.** Credibility rests on agreement across sources — and every domain has a catastrophe where that agreement was an echo of a *single origin*: the Iraq/Curveball circular-reporting failure, Wikipedia citogenesis, blockchain oracle laundering. You must count distinct **origin** nodes, not feeds.

The sharpest correction to the original thesis: **"cockpit-grade" is most often mis-read as MORE INFORMATION, and every domain proves the opposite.** The most expensive documented failure is not bad data but the *burial of the critical fact in a flat wall of true-but-trivial facts* (Air Canada 759 came within a small margin of catastrophe because a real, valid runway-closure NOTAM was lost mid-briefing — the NTSB cited *"ineffective presentation"*), and the *desensitization of an over-used alert channel* (clinicians override 49–96% of drug-interaction alerts; severity-tiering raised compliance by alerting **less**). 

> ⚠️ **Verification note on the alarm-tiering figure.** The widely-quoted "tripled clinical compliance by alerting less" rests on a single 2009 JAMIA study (Paterno et al.: 29% tiered vs 10% non-tiered overall acceptance; 100% vs 34% for the most-severe tier). It is real and directional but is one study under one EHR — treat "roughly tripled" as *illustrative of the mechanism*, not a transferable multiplier.

So the real teaching is: build a **calm utility whose discipline is REFUSAL** — refuse to fuse the two axes, refuse to let the verdict decide, refuse to interrupt without an action, refuse to count echoing sources, refuse to let a fact outlive its freshness window silently. The documented enemy is twofold: the committed human pushing through (plan-continuation bias) and the noisy channel that trained them to stop reading.

> ⚠️ **Verification note on plan-continuation bias.** The "~75%" figure is verified *for tactical/decision errors specifically* (NASA Ames; Orasanu/Berman/Dismukes review of NTSB accidents) — not for all accident causation. The companion claim that "over half of fatal weather accidents had advance warning" could **not** be verified against a primary source and must not be cited as a discrete statistic. Frame plan-continuation as "the dominant aviation *decision* error," not a precise share of all accidents.

The single most load-bearing engineering bet — and the hardest-to-copy moat — is the one only a provenance **graph** can make: walk the lineage to distinct origins so corroboration is real, propagate staleness and grants along those same edges, and keep the slow substrate immutable while ephemera stay JIT. The four-move architecture is, literally, a graph-traversal architecture. A subscription incumbent on a relational conditions table physically cannot walk lineage to distinct origins; their "corroborated" is our "echo." **The white space is no longer "features no one combines" but "a discipline no engagement-funded incumbent can adopt without contradicting their business model, expressed as a provenance-graph architecture they don't have."**

## 5. Per-lens deep dives

### Lens A — High-stakes go/no-go under uncertainty

**Domains:** aviation flight planning & dispatch, NOTAM overload, marine passage planning (CATZOC/APEM), avalanche forecasting (CMAH), clinical decision support (GRADE + alarms), search-and-rescue, wildland fire.

---

**PR-01 / CDP-09 — Personal minimums: capability-calibrated floors, set calm, tightened by transient factors.**
*What it is.* The FAA general-aviation method: a pilot sets per-category go/no-go floors (ceiling, visibility, wind, terrain) **buffered above the legal minimum** — "the human-factors equivalent of reserve fuel" — then applies a fixed additive adjustment table when a transient PAVE factor appears (illness/fatigue → +500 ft ceiling, +½ sm visibility; unfamiliar terrain → −5 kt acceptable wind). Step 6 is literally *"Stick to the Plan"*: minimums are fixed on the ground, not renegotiated in flight. *(All adjustment-table values verified against FAA personal-minimums worksheets.)*
*Why it transfers.* This is invariant 2's "confidence sets a floor, never the ranking" in mature form, and the template for Epic 007: Body Battery becomes a transient Pilot-factor that *tightens* the floor for the session. *UX:* a per-user "My limits" sheet seeded from logged outcomes, the binding floor named in one line, limits editable only from a calm planning context. *Disposition:* adapt. *Effort/leverage:* medium/high. *Eval:* A/B additive-floor vs blended score; no surfaced trip exceeds the user's adjusted limit (count violations, target zero).

**PR-02 / CDP-04 — FRAT/PAVE: structured multi-factor score → banded verdict with a prescribed action per band.**
*What it is.* The FAA Flight Risk Assessment Tool sums weighted hazards into a green/yellow/red band, each band carrying an *action* (proceed / mitigate-or-consult / cancel-unless-mitigated). The FAA is explicit it *"is not designed to make the go/no-go decision for you."* *(Verified; band labels vary across FRAT versions — don't over-specify wording.)*
*Why it transfers.* The exact shape of the Curator's verdict: synthesize many factors into ONE legible verdict carrying its binding constraint and a next action. *Disposition:* adopt. *Eval:* calibration test — bad-outcome trips should have carried MARGINAL/NO-GO verdicts with the correct binding constraint named.

**PR-03 / CDP-11 — Dispatch dual-concurrence + monitor-and-recall.**
*What it is.* Under 14 CFR 121.533, a flight may not depart unless both pilot-in-command and ground dispatcher independently concur; the dispatcher retains a duty to monitor and cancel/re-dispatch en route. *(Verified verbatim.)* *Why it transfers.* Separation-of-powers for the engine (Verifier = dispatcher synthesizing live overlays into a release; user = PIC) plus a monitor-and-recall half: live overlays keep refreshing after the plan is set. *Disposition:* adapt. *Eval:* inject a degraded overlay after clearance; system must issue a recall, never serve the stale release.

**PR-04 — Plan-continuation bias / get-there-itis (cautionary).** The dominant aviation *decision* error (~75% of tactical decision errors, NASA Ames — verified for decision errors only). The product's low-friction commit + live degradation is the exact trap. *Guardrail:* lock limits calm-and-early; frame recalls FORWARD ("best call from here"); make overriding a tightened floor a visible logged act; add zero engagement/streak pressure.

**PR-05 / CDP-05 — Criticality as a machine-readable first-class field (AIXM Digital NOTAM).**
*What it is.* The NOTAM fix is not prettier text — it is structured Digital NOTAM data carrying typed "event scenarios" (runway closure, obstacle) so downstream tools can suppress/differentiate by operational impact. The FAA's modernized service emits the data but deliberately does *not* relevance-rank — it separates the verified-data layer from the opinionated-presentation layer. *(Verified; "scope/traffic/purpose qualifiers" belong to the legacy ICAO Q-code, not the AIXM spec — a precision note.)*
*Why it transfers.* A flat, equally-weighted list of true facts reproduces NOTAM-blindness — the Air Canada 759 failure. Attach a criticality tag computed at the data layer that drives visual weight. *Disposition:* adopt. *⚠️ This collides with the shipped design system (see §7) — surface as a blocking open question, not a clean adopt.* *Eval:* one safety-critical overlay among N benign facts must take the top visual slot 100% of the time; median actionable flags/trip ≤1.

**PR-09 / CDP-09 — CATZOC error-budget margin: confidence inflates the safety buffer, never the ranking.**
*What it is.* Every charted area carries a confidence band (A1>A2>B>C>D, plus U=Unassessed). The depth-accuracy figure is a 95%-confidence error budget (`d_acc = a + b%·depth`) the navigator *subtracts* from charted depth to get a worst-case value used in the under-keel-clearance decision. Lower confidence → bigger allowance → more conservative call, but the area stays navigable and shown. *(Formulas and 2.45σ/2.00σ multipliers verified against IHO S-67.)*
*Why it transfers.* The rigorous form of invariant 2: confidence becomes a per-fact *margin* applied at the decision boundary, not a candidate demotion. A low-confidence water report widens the planning buffer (carry more water, add bail-out time), not the trail's feed rank. *Disposition:* adopt. *Eval:* two trips with identical desirability but different data confidence must NOT reorder; the lower-confidence one shows a larger safety buffer.

**PR-10 / CDP-02 — Assessed-low vs Unassessed (U) as distinct states.** CATZOC's `U` ("never evaluated") is a different symbol from `D` ("poor, but assessed"). Both differ from a confidently-surveyed shoal. *Why it transfers.* Invariant 1 made operational: never collapse "low-confidence fact" and "no data." A spring with a stale low-authority report (assessed-low) is categorically different from one never verified (silence). *Disposition:* adopt. *Eval:* every user-facing fact maps to exactly one of {stated+sourced, hedged-low, unassessed-silence}.

**PR-11 / CDP-11 — APEM lifecycle with continuous monitoring + look-ahead.** SOLAS Ch.V codifies Appraisal→Planning→Execution→Monitoring; ECDIS projects a look-ahead anti-grounding cone that re-alarms when the predicted path crosses the safety contour. *(Verified.)* The safety check re-runs continuously as live data arrives, and the look-ahead horizon is a tunable per-stage parameter — directly the Verifier-freshness windows. *Disposition:* adapt.

**PR-12 / CDP-08 — Notices-to-Mariners freshness taxonomy.** Chart corrections are typed Permanent / Temporary / Preliminary, each with different lifetime and trust; the navigator stays responsible for currency, and T&P notices are handled *inconsistently across providers* — a coverage gap that is itself a provenance fact. *Disposition:* adapt → per-data-type freshness windows + source-disagreement disclosure.

**PR-13 / CDP-04/06 — CMAH bottom-up hazard composition.** Avalanche forecasting (Statham et al. 2018, verified) builds hazard from named "avalanche problems," each carrying likelihood (= sensitivity × distribution) and size, rolled up into one danger rating that stays *traceable to its named parts*. *Why it transfers.* The shape of the Curator's verdict: go/marginal/no-go is the roll-up of named, individually-sourced concerns, and the headline is always re-derivable from its parts.

**PR-14 / CDP-07 — Confidence/uncertainty as a separate axis.** Avalanche forecasts carry a confidence rating *orthogonal* to the hazard rating, defined by data-quality + corroboration + predictability, with the explicit rule *"collecting different data reduces uncertainty, more of the same does not"* — a precise statement of the corroboration dimension. *(Verified.)*

**PR-15 / CDP-04 — Hazard ≠ risk.** *"Avalanche hazard is Mother Nature"*; risk only exists once you expose something to it. The forecast stops at hazard; terrain/party/exposure are the user's. *Why it transfers.* Keeps a calm utility from becoming a presumptuous oracle: state the verified conditions authoritatively, let the user apply their own exposure. Over-claiming a personalized "no-go" on thin preference data repeats the failure the doctrine forbids.

**PR-17/PR-18 / CDP-04/07 — GRADE two-axis verdict + strength-as-linguistic-contract.** GRADE splits evidence-certainty (high/moderate/low) from recommendation-strength (strong/weak), deliberately decoupled: a strong recommendation can ride on low-certainty evidence (rapid antibiotics in sepsis), a weak one on high-certainty (a proven-but-tiny benefit where patient values dominate). UpToDate binds strength to fixed verbs — *"We recommend"* (strong) vs *"We suggest"* (weak). *(Verified against the GRADE handbook.)* *Why it transfers.* The formal model behind invariant 2 and the contract for GO-vs-MARGINAL: a STRONG GO is licensed only when robust to plausible preference perturbations.

**PR-19 / CDP-15 — Severity-tiered friction for warnings (cautionary anchor).** Clinicians override 49–96% of drug-interaction alerts; undifferentiated high-volume alerting *desensitizes*. The documented fix is severity-tiering. *(Override range and tiering effect verified directionally; the "tripled compliance" multiplier is one 2009 study — treat as illustrative, see §2.)*

**PR-20 / CDP-17 — Hedge by softening + safety-netting, not confessing ignorance.** Vignette research (Bhise et al. 2018, verified) found *explicit* "I'm not sure" lowers trust, while *implicit* hedging ("most likely X, but watch for Y") plus a concrete follow-up plan preserves trust and honesty. *(The "safety-net reverses the harm" claim must be attributed to a separate 2023 review, not the Bhise study, and softened to "mitigates.")* *Why it transfers.* The calm-utility sweet spot: lead with the best-supported read, disclose what's thin, convert the gap into a concrete next checkpoint.

**PR-21 / CDP-07/10 — Two-factor POS = POC × POD.** SAR's core arithmetic separates "where it might be" (POC, desirability analogue) from "how well we looked" (POD, verification analogue); only their product measures effectiveness. *(Verified against USCG Theory of Search.)* *Why it transfers.* Invariant 2 as arithmetic — desirability and confidence stay orthogonal, and an unsuccessful search renormalizes the prior multiplicatively (POC₁ = POC₀·(1−POD)) rather than zeroing it: the model for a failed/empty live call (downgrade confidence + disclose, never fabricate or silently drop).

**PR-23 — Open-system Rest-Of-World residual mass.** Land-SAR added an explicit ROW segment holding the residual probability the subject is *outside* everything mapped, preventing false completeness. *Why it transfers.* The curated feed must never imply exhaustiveness — keep a non-zero "none of these / we can't confidently match yet" state.

**PR-24 — Independent-then-aggregate priors + GOA×GOD false-precision (cautionary).** Mattson consensus elicits expert priors *privately and independently* (to stop vocal members anchoring meeker ones) — good. But the "Pseudoscience of Land Search" critique shows averaged subjective opinions get laundered into precise-looking probabilities (GOA×GOD = GOS). *Guardrail:* inferred priors carry coarse low/med/high + an inference tag, never spurious decimals; and authority is per-domain, not transferable (the maritime→land transplant error = the watch as physiology sensor laundered into a preference claim).

**PR-25 / CDP-11 — Trigger points as predetermined re-evaluation cues.** NWCG doctrine: trigger points are *"predetermined cues to prompt you to re-evaluate,"* set during planning, explicitly *not decision-makers.* *(Verified verbatim.)* *Why it transfers.* Each binding constraint shows its trigger as a labeled threshold set calm; a crossed trigger flips the status and surfaces a recall.

**PR-27 / CDP-11 — LCES/GeoLCES: safety as a perishable geospatial state.** LCES (Lookouts/Communications/Escape routes/Safety zones) must be *"continuously evaluated and re-established as conditions change"*; GeoLCES operationalizes it as lidar-derived 30 m metrics. *(Verified.)* *Why it transfers.* Trip safety is perishable: Lookouts = live sources, Communications = a fresh fetch, Escape Routes = bail-outs, Safety Zones = the abort plan — recomputed on each JIT refresh.

**PR-121 / CDP-02/05 — Dark-cockpit / attention-by-exception.** Nominal = quiet; pixels light up only on deviation. *(Verified, Airbus origin.)* The structural form of source-or-silence + calm: a quiet field must mean "checked and nominal," rendered *distinctly* from "unchecked/unreachable" (the AF447 mode-confusion lesson). *Disposition:* adopt.

**PR-122/PR-125 / CDP-05/15 — FAA 25.1322 alert hierarchy + IEC 60601-1-8 two-axis priority.** Aviation mandates three tiers (Warning/Caution/Advisory) with reserved color, single-aural-arbitration (only the most urgent speaks), and two-sense cuing. *(Verified — but "only one aural alert at a time" and "green reserved for normal" are Advisory-Circular guidance/convention, NOT the binding regulation's wording; the reg says advisory may be "any color except red or green.")* Clinical alarm priority is severity × *onset-time-to-harm* — a flash-flood watch with hours of lead time is a different priority than an active warning at equal severity. *Why it transfers.* The grammar for a {GO, MARGINAL, NO-GO} verdict that names one binding constraint, with severity auto-triggered by measured thresholds for hard facts and conservatively rendered for inferred ones.

**PR-123 — Ecological Interface Design.** Rasmussen/Vicente: build the display from the work-domain's *constraint structure* so operators can reason through novel situations the designer never anticipated. *Why it transfers.* Paint the binding constraint as a perceptual *margin band* (a daylight bar showing finish-vs-sunset; an effort gauge showing demand vs the readiness floor) so when a live overlay shifts, the margin visibly shrinks — making the user smarter at the re-plan rather than more dependent on a verdict.

**PR-124 — Ironies of Automation (cautionary).** Bainbridge: automating the easy parts atrophies the skill needed for the hard parts and breeds complacency. Empirically: glass-cockpit light aircraft had a *higher* fatal-accident rate than steam-gauge equivalents (1.03 vs 0.43 per 100k hours, verified). AF447: the stall warning activated ~75 times but, cutting in and out, was disbelieved. *Guardrail:* never let high confidence read as "you don't need to think"; hedge on novel/edge cases; make safety flags *sticky* (degrade to "last verified Xh ago," never flap on/off — an intermittent warning trains disbelief).

**PR-126/PR-127 / CDP-15 — Alarm rationalization (the actionability gate) + false-positive suppression.** ISA-18.2: an alarm earns the right to interrupt ONLY if it has a documented cause, consequence, time-to-respond, and a corrective action the user can take; 85–99% of clinical alarms need no intervention, so rationalization removes the noise at the source. *(Verified.)* Persistence delays + multi-parameter corroboration cut false alarms 50–67% without missing true events. *(Delay figures verified to Goerges 2009, NOT the cited Chambrin paper; the "63% at Johns Hopkins SpO2" figure is unverified/conflated — the isolated 90%→88% change yields ~43–45%.)*

**PR-128 — Fail-safe legibility (cautionary).** 80 of 98 Joint Commission alarm sentinel events were *deaths*, the mechanism near-always self-inflicted desensitization from non-actionable noise → silencing → missing the real one. *(Verified.)* *Guardrail:* low confidence changes *presentation* (hedge) and adds a *safety flag*, but never deletes a high-severity signal; a stale source fails loud, never to a reassuring quiet. There is no "blocked" state, only hedged/flagged/silent.

**PR-129/PR-130/PR-132 / CDP-03/05/02 — Impact-Based Warning wire format, refuse-the-number, finite vocabulary.** NWS IBW encodes every alert as Hazard + Source + Impact with a machine-readable source tag ("radar indicated" vs "observed") — provenance and capability-vs-confidence as a wire format. MBTA refuses to invent an ETA it can't support, downgrading to verifiable status ("N stops away") or a structural fallback (average headway) — degrade-and-disclose down a ladder (live → status → corpus-structural → labeled-silence), each rung still true. The NWS Hazard Simplification collapse (a homepage map that grew to 122 colors, found confusing, collapsing to 2 plain-language tiers) is both an adopt-pattern (finite vocabulary) and a cautionary failure (uncontrolled category proliferation destroys glanceability). *(All verified, with sourcing caveats noted.)*

**PR-131 — Proximity-bucketed, asymmetric accuracy tolerance (Transit ETA benchmark).** Tolerance *tightens as the event nears* and is *asymmetric* — being early (the dangerous optimistic miss) is punished harder than being late. *(Verified exactly against the benchmark.)* *Why it transfers.* The Epic 009 loss function: a "predicted-safe, actually-hazardous" miss must score strictly worse than an equal-magnitude conservative miss, and the trust threshold to emit a hard verdict should tighten as days-to-departure shrink.

## 6. UX implications (consolidated)

> Surface-by-surface. Then overarching principles. Focused, not over-indexed.

**Feed card.** Two structurally separate readouts that never multiply into one star: feed ORDER set by desirability/fit only (the shipped Confidence/Staleness treatment already demotes *presentation* without reordering — so enforce this as a regression assertion, not aspiration), and a dedicated confidence chip carrying the certainty axis. A superb trail with thin live data sits at rank #1 wearing an amber "Unverified" chip. Criticality (not recency or list order) drives visual weight: the killer fact survives a 3-second glance, trivia collapses into a muted tray, and a trip with zero binding constraints shows a clean verdict and NO flags (absence of alarms is itself trustworthy signal).

**Detail map.** A closure paints hard red, an advisory a thin amber hairline; a silence segment paints a neutral caution stripe (never absence, never a confident dot). Live overlays render as feathered *bands* whose fuzziness encodes confidence (isochrone artifacts, snap ambiguity), never crisp authoritative lines. An EID constraint band (daylight margin vs sunset; effort vs readiness floor) *moves* as a live overlay shifts, so the user sees the margin shrink and can drag start-time to recover it. "No connected path in our data (segment isolated; last ingested DATE). We won't guess."

**Verdict line (new surface, atop feed card + Detail head).** One line in fixed {GO, MARGINAL, NO-GO} language naming exactly ONE binding constraint as a tap-through to its live source+timestamp; always overridable (an override is recorded as preference signal); contributing factors stay visible below, never hidden behind the score. It reads "No-go on current conditions, because X" (auditable), never "Don't go" (command). GRADE recommend/suggest maps to GO/MARGINAL. Never GO when a binding input is silent — degrade to "Marginal — unverified" naming the silent input.

**Confidence chip.** Two things, never one fused number: a source/authority badge AND a corroboration+freshness state ("USGS gauge, confirmed vs NWS" vs "USGS gauge, single source, 6h old"). A first-class fourth treatment splits *unassessed-silence* (tombstone: identity + last value + timestamp + reason) from *assessed-low*. Corroboration is honest about independence ("2 feeds, shared origin — counts as 1"). On tap, decomposes per-attribute (card chip = weakest-link MIN; reliable attributes stay green).

**Tuning.** "My limits" sheet (max gain/day, distance, exposure, daylight buffer, ford/snow/heat thresholds) seeded from logged outcomes, presented as the buffer ABOVE the hard line — editable ONLY from a calm planning context, never in-trip. Two panels: "what you told us" separate from a quiet "what we've noticed"; any inferred contradiction is a hedged, dismissable QUESTION; the aspirational stated goal is never deleted. Readiness tightening shown as a hedged band with reason, never a precise "87% match," never a hard veto. Zero engagement framing: choosing easier/resting/turning-back is always frictionless and never negatively framed.

**Alert / notification.** Two visibly separate channels. INTERRUPT (push/banner) reserved for rationalized, actionable, time-bound items each stating a corrective verb ("Renew permit by 6pm"); everything else is a quiet IN-CONTEXT fact. A "why am I seeing this?" affordance reveals cause/consequence/action. Sticky flags degrade to "last verified Xh ago," never flap; a muted hazard keeps a persistent "hazards muted" indicator. Severity × time-to-onset drives the channel.

**Outcome.** A correction layers, never replaces ("You logged: felt like Class III, not II — your note, 06-20" beneath the corpus rating, both visible). A past trip shows "Verdict as evaluated 2026-06-12 14:30" exactly as it stood. A save is captured as a noisy positive, never a confident preference.

**Overarching principles.**
1. **Two axes, never one number** — desirability sets order; confidence sets the chip, the buffer, and the safety flag, in separate registers. Regression assertion, not guideline.
2. **Silence is a designed, loud, distinct state** — never a blank spinner; quiet must mean "checked and nominal," visibly distinct from unchecked/stale.
3. **One auditable advisory verdict, never an oracle** — one binding constraint, tap-to-source, overridable, factors never hidden.
4. **Earn the right to interrupt** — cause + consequence + time + corrective verb, or it's a quiet fact.
5. **Criticality drives visual weight, not recency.**
6. **Capability ≠ preference, rendered in two registers** — an inference never wears a measurement's clothing.
7. **Confidence widens a buffer, never a guarantee, never a penalty.**
8. **Keep the vocabulary tiny and fixed** — verdicts ≤3, confidence bands ≤3; add nothing without retiring something.
9. **Lock limits calm-and-early; frame recalls FORWARD.**
10. **Add zero engagement pressure.** Calm is a *safety* property here, not an aesthetic.
11. **Share the conclusion, never the substrate; enforce it below the agent.**
12. **Show your work; honest hedging earns trust** — corroboration counts distinct origins, uncertainty shows as a band, never false precision.

> ⚠️ **The single largest unaddressed UX collision (critic, must surface as a blocking open question).** CDP-04's GO/MARGINAL/NO-GO chip and CDP-05's criticality-driven red/amber visual weight **directly contradict the ratified, WCAG-AA-audited `design-system-v0.1.md §7.3`**, which lists as *Forbidden*: "banner, modal, toast, stoplight/severity palette, color-scream," and states the signal hue is "the ONLY place the signal hue appears." A "red impact band pins the binding hazard to the top" and per-level red/amber map painting *is* a stoplight/severity palette by another name. The shipped Signal component's absence of a severity scale is a deliberate, documented, safety-reasoned decision — not an oversight. **This must be resolved before any "adopt" is acted on**, via one of: (a) a formal §10-governed design-contract amendment with the Air Canada 759 / alarm-fatigue evidence as justification, or (b) a redesign of criticality as *structural weight without a severity palette* (size/position/typography rather than color). The §4.3/§14 colorblind-safe contract ("color never carries the message by itself") further requires any criticality encoding to pair color with a non-color cue. **Treat CDP-05 (and the verdict chip's color) as an open design question, not a clean adopt.**

> ⚠️ **Anonymous + web-push degradation paths (critic, must specify).** Anonymous browsing of the world + live conditions is a *first-class product* (CLAUDE.md), and reliable push is a native-iOS-later capability — on web/PWA there is no reliable background recall. So: (a) every decision surface (verdict, floor, watch-recall, taste) needs an explicit no-overlay / no-grants fallback — a verdict naming a binding constraint relative to "your limit" is meaningless with no limits on file, so the anonymous verdict must fall back to absolute conditions only; (b) CDP-11's forward-framed recall — the headline safety move — has no delivery surface in the shipped web product; surface this dependency rather than assuming push.

## 7. Cautionary-failures catalog

| failure | domain(s) | the guardrail for us |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Fuse-and-gate** — collapsing confidence factors into one number/badge/color, OR letting that number demote/gate/throttle a candidate (documented in FOUR domains: Admiralty diagonal-collapse; NewsGuard score-as-chokepoint; blockchain provenance-theater; citator green-light fallacy). *The most expensive mistake in the provenance corpus.* | Provenance/trust | NEVER render confidence as a single fused number/letter/star/color; show decomposed (authority badge + corroboration+freshness state). Confidence may set a FLOOR, hedge presentation, raise a safety flag — never demote/gate/throttle. No "blocked" state, only hedged/flagged/silent. Epic 009 regression: low-confidence data on a high-desirability trail must NOT change rank. |
| **Corroboration without independence** — counting echoing feeds that share one origin as mutual confirmation (Iraq Curveball; Wikipedia citogenesis; "two sources = two beliefs"; blockchain oracle laundering — 5 domains). | Provenance + connected-data | Walk provenance edges to distinct ORIGIN nodes before crediting any corroboration boost; confidence = MIN across the chain, never average; collapse shared-origin agreement to single-source in the UI; never re-ingest the engine's own output as external evidence. |
| **Undifferentiated presentation of real facts** — a flat equally-weighted list where the killer fact drowns (Air Canada 759; NTSB cited "ineffective presentation"). | NOTAM + clinical | Make criticality a first-class machine-readable field driving visual weight (CDP-05); assume the user will NOT read carefully. **⚠️ See §6 — the criticality encoding collides with the shipped design system; resolve as a contract amendment, not a quiet adopt.** |
| **Alarm/alert fatigue** — finite credibility budget; every non-actionable alert spends it (clinical 49–96% override; ECDIS groundings from disabled-because-noisy alarms). *Every false NO-GO push trains users to mute the one that matters — the death mode.* | Calm + high-stakes | Gate every interrupt on actionability (cause + consequence + time + corrective verb); demote the rest to ambient. Track heed-rate vs interruptive-volume — a negative slope means failure rebuilt. |
| **Hard-stops backfire** — blocking alerts with no override produced therapeutic delays (an RCT terminated early), workarounds, falsified scores, paradoxically MORE alerts. | Clinical | Reserve true blocks for near-absolute cases (active closures/evac); everything else is an overridable warning naming its reason. | **Oracle creep** — letting a risk-scoring tool make the call (doctrinally forbidden: FAA FRAT; avalanche hazard ≠ risk). A verdict that hides its inputs manufactures false confidence; one wrong verdict collapses trust in ALL future ones. | Aviation, avalanche | Always name the single binding constraint, link to its live source, stay overridable, never hide factors. "No-go on current conditions, because X," never "Don't go." |

| **False precision** — rendering a soft inferred prior as a confident number (SAR's GOA×GOD averaged to two decimals; Hodgson's same-wine Bronze-to-Gold). | SAR + sensory science | Inferred beliefs carry coarse low/med/high + an inference tag, never spurious decimals; "high confidence" is never a warranty (prefer "verified 22 min ago"). Gate any point-score behind a replicate-reproducibility test. |
| **Silent degradation** — the system keeps operating on a picture it no longer has, and absence reads as calm (Yarnell communications gap of **over 30 minutes**; Palantir ontology stale within months; "water at mile 6" true last season now dangerous). *Stale data is worse than absent because it commands false trust.* | High-stakes + connected-data | Missing/stale data must be loud (tombstone, CDP-02); a binding input that goes silent pushes the verdict to "unverified" and names itself. Per-data-type freshness windows; a quiet field renders distinctly from unchecked/unreachable (AF447). |
| **Substrate-corruption-before-confidence** — transitive-closure over loose match edges chain-fuses distinct trails into one node (invisible at the pair level), then closures/streamflow/permits attach to the WRONG real-world place. *Highest-stakes failure for a safety utility — corrupts the substrate BEFORE confidence is computed.* | Connected-data (ER, FamilySearch, snap) | Never auto-fuse on weak transitive chains; threshold + degree/size guards + review cap; merges additive + reversible; require negative evidence to break chains; attach a confidence to the MERGE/SNAP itself. **Directly applies to the live 1643→1458 slug-merge audit.** |
| **The new-enemy problem** — eventual-consistency caching of the access wrapper is unsafe for revocations; a revoke that syncs after new sensitive content, or stale policy on new content, leaks data even when tuples are current. | Connected-data (Zanzibar/SpiceDB) | Bind every derived conclusion/grant to the data-version token it assumed; re-evaluate share visibility at ≥ the content's version (`at_least_as_fresh`). Only the slow public corpus may use relaxed/cached consistency; the private overlay never may. |

Silently overwriting the aspirational self breaches trust. | Capability vs preference (dating) | Require a multivariate, pool-controlled contradiction before even hedging; keep stated and revealed as separate provenance-tagged channels; present any inferred contradiction as a user-confirmable question; never delete the stated goal. |

| **Automation complacency / mode confusion** — automating the easy case atrophies skill for the hard case; intermittent safety signals get disbelieved (AF447's ~75-activation stall warning cutting in and out; glass cockpits' higher fatal rate). | Calm + aviation | Hedge on novel/edge cases; make safety flags sticky ("last verified Xh ago," never flapping); always make the system's "mode" legible (what's verified, assumed, unseeable). - **Calibration metric (missing today).** Add proper-scoring-rule / Brier / reliability-diagram scoring alongside the structure-vs-baseline A/B. ## 10. Open questions (for the founder + design team)

1. **Verdict authority calibration (CDP-04).** GRADE licenses a STRONG verdict only when robust to plausible preference perturbations — but what is the actual perturbation test for a hike, and what % of nearby preference draws must a GO survive? Until specified, "recommend vs suggest" is a label, not a contract. *Needs a design session before the verdict layer ships.*
2. **Freshness-window numbers (CDP-08).** The per-data-type windows (weather/streamflow/AQI/permits/fire/terrain) are unset, and the Stage-4 cost spike is unmeasured. Windows must be set against BOTH the real Anthropic query-time cost (now measurable on live Aura) and each fact's true volatility. Who owns this calibration, and does it block the verdict layer?
3. **Origin-metadata feasibility (CDP-01 — the moat's precondition).** Do NWS/USGS/AirNow/RIDB/FIRMS expose enough origin metadata to tell shared-model-run from independent observation? If origin is unrecoverable for a source pair, the safe default is **treat-as-shared (conservative)** — write it down. *The entire moat claim is conditional on this; gate CDP-01 (and CDP-19) on a feasibility spike before treating them as clean adopts.*
4. **Solo-vs-party readiness composition (CDP-09 / Epic 007).** Whose readiness floor binds a shared trip, and how does that interact with the grant boundary (one person's deadline must never override another's safety floor, Inv 5)? Plus: validate Body Battery as a hiking-effort predictor before it tightens a safety floor.
5. **Slug-merge audit promotion (CDP-14).** The 1643→1458 slug-merge in the loaded Shenandoah region is exactly the substrate-corruption-before-confidence risk — ~185 collapsed canonical_ids may be same-trail OR collision, and a wrong fuse means a future overlay attaches to the wrong real place. Should the merge audit be **promoted to blocking before live-data dogfood**, given it corrupts the substrate beneath every downstream confidence claim?
6. **Is the discipline decorative? (Epic 009).** Be prepared for the finding that some of this discipline shows no measured accuracy gain (Dhami 2024 / RAND). Which patterns are we willing to cut if the eval says they are decorative?
7. **Criticality tag assignment (CDP-05).** Who assigns the {closure/hazard/capacity/advisory} tag for a given source response — is it derivable mechanically (InciWeb closure flag, RIDB quota=0) or does it need a per-data-type rule the Verifier-freshness epic must author? *And the design-system collision in §6 must be resolved first.*
8. **Watch-snap confidence gate before influence (CDP-20).** The always-on watch-poller host is the still-deferred R7 piece. Does the HMM-snap confidence gate need to exist before ANY watch track influences taste/readiness, to prevent a low-confidence snap silently attributing an outcome to the wrong trail?
