# UI Brief v0.2

> **STATUS: IMPLEMENTED (PR #22)** — the north-star posture here shipped in the personal-intelligence app UX (Home/Curation). Kept as the UX brief / visual-language reference. **Read when** designing app UX or visual language.

## 1. Product Definition

- **What this is**
  - An **outdoor intelligence** product for hiking decisions.

- **What that means**
  - It synthesizes:
    - place
    - conditions
    - constraints
    - personal context
  - into a **small number of strong, usable options**.

- **What it is not**
  - Not a social feed.
  - Not a generic chat-AI app.
  - Not a fitness dashboard.
  - Not a filter-heavy trail search tool.

## 2. Core Product Posture

- **Primary posture**
  - **Curation**, not browsing.
  - **Intelligence**, not assistant theater.
  - **Selection**, not endless exploration.

- **User relationship**
  - The product should feel like a **restrained, intelligent selector**.
  - It should have point of view, but not personality theater.

- **Job to be done**
  - Help me find a small set of compelling hike options that are actually viable for this moment.

## 3. Experience Intent

- **Main emotional target**
  - The product should feel:
    - **tasteful + editorial**
    - **precise + lucid**
    - **restrained + humane**

- **What that excludes**
  - Not chatty
  - Not enthusiastic
  - Not “outdoors lifestyle”
  - Not technical for technicality’s sake
  - Not sterile luxury minimalism

- **Best shorthand**
  - **Quiet premium utility**

## 4. Design Principles

- **Curation over feed**
  - The interface should never feel like a stream.
  - It should feel finite, deliberate, and opinionated.

- **Progressive tuning**
  - The experience starts calm and curated.
  - More control appears as needed.
  - It should not feel like a control panel up front.

- **Place before explanation**
  - Recommendations should first help me sense the place.
  - Explanation and evidence deepen as I inspect.

- **Practicality before ceremony**
  - On detail, the product must quickly answer:
    - can I actually do this today?

- **Trust should be subtle on the main surface**
  - Trust is crucial, but primary surfaces should not get badge-heavy or bureaucratic.
  - Trust should mostly come through:
    - copy
    - hierarchy
    - freshness cues
    - reveal structure

- **Personalization should be effect-first**
  - The main experience should show why something fits.
  - It should not foreground the machinery of personal inference.

## 5. Trust Model

- **Trust signals exist**
  - but should be **quietly embedded**, not loudly labeled by default.

- **Primary trust primitives**
  - confidence
  - freshness/staleness
  - verify-before-you-go

- **How they behave**
  - On primary surfaces:
    - mostly implicit
    - structurally legible
    - not badge-led
  - On deeper surfaces:
    - more inspectable
    - more explicit
    - easier to interrogate

- **Personalization transparency**
  - The product should mostly say:
    - “this fits”
  - rather than:
    - “here is the model of you”
  - The deeper belief/receipt layer exists, but it is not the center of the daily experience.

## 6. Main Surface: Curation

- **Name**
  - The main surface is best framed as **Curation**.

- **What it should optimize for**
  - Not passive browsing.
  - Not pure editorial consumption.
  - Not instant commitment.
  - It should support **deliberate tuning inside a composed selection**.

- **Structure**
  - A **hybrid header**:
    - compact context
    - tuning controls
    - secondary free-text
  - followed by a very small set of recommendations

- **Input posture**
  - Free text is present, but **secondary**.
  - The home surface should not feel chat-first.

- **Result set**
  - **3 or fewer** active recommendations.
  - They should feel **lightly ordered**, not aggressively ranked.

## 7. Recommendation Card Model

- **At-rest density**
  - **Layered**
  - Calm at first glance, but clearly deeper than it first appears.

- **What a card should lead with**
  - **Mood / place first**
  - Not raw stats
  - Not immediate rationale
  - Not generic utility copy

- **What expansion reveals, in order**
  - **1. Terrain / map**
  - **2. Practical details**
  - **3. Why it fits**
  - **4. Trust details / source inspection**

- **Primary action**
  - **Open Detail**
  - The card is not mainly for immediate commitment or routing.

## 8. Detail Surface Model

- **Role of detail**
  - It is the inspection and decision surface.

- **What it should lead with**
  - **Practical viability first**
  - with map/terrain close behind

- **Core question detail answers**
  - Is this viable?
  - What kind of place is this?
  - What do I need to know?
  - Why was this selected?
  - Can I trust the conditions shown?

## 9. Personalization / Belief Layer

- **Belief store role**
  - **Occasional transparency layer**
  - Important, but not a primary daily destination

- **Why it exists**
  - To inspect, correct, and understand the system when needed

- **What it should not become**
  - A dashboard identity center
  - A constant self-modeling surface
  - A headline feature on the home experience

## 10. Visual Character

- **Overall visual world**
  - **Quiet premium utility**

- **Where the character comes from**
  - **Map / terrain cues**
  - **tight, tactile behavior**
  - not expressive motion
  - not photography-led mood

- **Place language**
  - Geographic
  - orienting
  - terrain-aware
  - not lifestyle scenic

- **Interaction feel**
  - **Tight + tactile**
  - crisp
  - responsive
  - controlled
  - premium without flourish

- **Palette**
  - **Monochrome + signal accents**
  - a neutral, disciplined base
  - accent only when meaningful

- **Typography**
  - **Cartographic modern**
  - precise
  - spatially aware
  - label-conscious
  - disciplined, not soft-humanist

## 11. What We Are Building First

- **First prototype**
  - **Home / Curation**

- **Prototype scope**
  - hybrid header
  - progressive tuning
  - 3-or-fewer recommendation set
  - layered card anatomy
  - map/terrain-led expansion
  - practical viability cues
  - subtle trust expression

- **What the prototype must test**
  - Does this feel like curation, not a feed?
  - Does it avoid generic chat-AI energy?
  - Does it feel premium without becoming aloof?
  - Does it feel place-aware without leaning on photography?
  - Does the tuning feel powerful without turning the surface into a search tool?

## 12. Design Tensions To Watch

- **Curation vs control**
  - Enough tuning to feel intelligent and precise
  - not so much that the home becomes a filter instrument

- **Place vs practicality**
  - Enough sense of place to create desire
  - enough viability to support real decisions

- **Subtle trust vs invisible trust**
  - Quiet trust cues are right
  - but they can’t become too hidden to matter

- **Premium vs personality vacuum**
  - Restraint is right
  - sterility is not

## 13. The Working One-Liner

- **Working one-liner**
  - A **quiet outdoor intelligence product** that curates a few viable hikes with taste, terrain awareness, and restrained trust.
