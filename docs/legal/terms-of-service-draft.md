## 1. What Adventure Planner is

Adventure Planner is a private hiking trip planner. It tells you whether a specific trip is a good idea right now, backs every part of that answer with a named source and a timestamp, and learns your preferences over time so the next answer is better.

It is a utility, not a social network. The success state is you closing the app and going outside.

## 3. Your hiking decisions are yours

> **Adventure Planner is an informational tool. It is not a substitute for your own judgment, official sources, or emergency services.**

The app provides advisory verdicts — go, marginal, or no-go — about trail conditions based on publicly available data. These verdicts are **informational only.** They are not guarantees of safety. Here is what that means:

- **You decide.** Every hiking decision and its associated risk are yours. The app scores; you decide.
- **Conditions change.** Trail conditions, weather, water levels, fire risk, air quality, and other factors change continuously. The app's information reflects what was available at the time it was fetched, which may already be outdated by the time you read it.
- **Sources have limits.** The app displays data from government agencies, weather services, and other public sources. These sources have their own accuracy limitations, reporting delays, and coverage gaps. The app discloses its sources and their freshness — take that disclosure seriously.
- **The app is not an emergency service.** Do not rely on Adventure Planner in an emergency. Call 911 or the appropriate emergency number.
- **Assume responsibility.** You are responsible for carrying appropriate gear, checking official sources (ranger stations, park service websites, local authorities), informing someone of your plans, and making conservative decisions when uncertain.

We present this disclaimer prominently because we take it seriously — not to distance ourselves from our product, but because honest uncertainty is the foundation of what we build.

## 7. What the service is not

- Adventure Planner is **not a map application.** It does not provide turn-by-turn navigation.
- Adventure Planner is **not a medical device.** Capability assessments from watch data are hedged estimates, not medical advice.
- Adventure Planner is **not a weather service.** Weather data comes from public feeds and is displayed with its source and fetch time. Always check official forecasts before heading out.
- Adventure Planner is **not a social network.** There are no public profiles, no followers, no feeds of other people's activity.
