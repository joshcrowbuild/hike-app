## Vision

A calm, private planner that tells one person — and their household — whether a specific trip is a good idea right now, and backs every word of that answer with a named source, a timestamp, and an honest confidence — so they can read it in thirty seconds, trust it under stress, and close the app and go outside.

## Thesis — cockpit-grade discipline to the trailhead

The bet is **"cockpit-grade discipline to the trailhead."** Our cross-domain research found that thirteen independent high-stakes disciplines (aviation, marine, avalanche, clinical, SAR, intelligence) converged — without coordinating — on the same four moves: keep desirability and confidence on two axes that never collapse; the tool scores but the human decides; safety is a perishable state re-established continuously; corroboration is the engine but independence is the governor.

The outdoor-app field is epistemically sloppy precisely because hiking stakes usually *feel* low: the competitive review found the entire market's dominant failure is showing undated, unsourced data as current truth, with best-in-class provenance existing only at the *feature* level (FarOut's water status), never the *app* level. So the white space is a fusion no incumbent attempts — and no engagement-funded incumbent *can* attempt without contradicting their business model: honest unified confidence + un-paywalled, un-staled live safety truth + outcome-learned **private** taste that separates capability from preference, on a finite calm surface. The deepest, hardest-to-copy form of this is a **provenance-graph architecture** that walks lineage along its own edges — a structural advantage a subscription incumbent on a relational conditions table physically cannot occupy. That graph owns transitive-staleness propagation and grant-on-edge semantics *unconditionally*; its strongest form — independence-checked corroboration ("their corroborated is our echo") — is **contingent** on whether the live feeds expose recoverable distinct-origin metadata, the one open question the path-to-complete front-loads a spike to settle. The moat is real either way; how loud the corroboration claim can be is what the spike decides.

We have already built the spine faithfully (taste-blind one-property confidence, source-or-silence at the Verifier, query-layer access control CI-proven with falsifiability tests, the `capability ≠ preference` Belief tags, the honesty-primitive UI). The bet now is to honor that discipline **all the way to a real user**: implement the corroboration we advertise (today a constant `1` — the exact feed-counting sin the research names as the field's cardinal failure), give the machinery a real intake (auth + episode creation + history import), and add the operational substrate a live multi-user safety utility requires, *before* widening the door.

## What "complete & deeply useful" means

A complete Adventure Planner is one where a real person can sign in, find any trail (near them now, or somewhere they're dreaming of from home), get an honest **go / marginal / no-go** read whose every fact carries a live source, a timestamp, and a confidence that genuinely reflects how many **independent** origins corroborate it — then log how the trip actually went, watch that outcome sharpen the next answer, and (when they choose) share the *derived conclusion* with a household member without ever leaking the raw substrate.

Concretely, "complete" requires four things today's beautiful prototype lacks:

1. **A real intake under the personal-intelligence machinery.** Managed per-user auth replacing the single shared dev-secret; an *authenticated HTTP* path to create episodes (today creation exists only as a batch FIT-ingest stub — `ingestion/ingest_episode.py` → `queries.upsert_episode` — with no authed endpoint over HTTP; nothing creates an Episode for a real signed-in user); history import (GPX/Strava/Garmin) as the `been_on` producer that pre-warms taste and unblocks novelty. Without it the entire belief / context / novelty / outcome loop runs on mock data for one seeded user.
2. **The correctness the product advertises.** Independence-checked corroboration that walks to distinct origin nodes (CDP-01); an advisory verdict layer naming one binding constraint with its source (CDP-04); per-data-type freshness windows with loud, four-state silence (CDP-02/08); and a substrate provably not silently fusing two real trails into one (the unaudited 1643→1458 conflation delta + the narrow but unguarded same-source ≤40-char slug-collision case, promoted to BLOCKING).
3. **The table-stakes surface every real app needs.** Search/geocoding; more than one verifiable region; onboarding; settings; offline-aware freshness; and the four legible empty-states that keep an honest app in a sparse region from reading as broken.
4. **The operational substrate a live, publicly-reachable, health-adjacent app demands.** Rate-limiting on the LLM-and-live-API-fanning `/plan` endpoint; observability and cost metrics; a ToS / privacy / consent surface; data export and deletion; and an automated regression gate (golden trips + cassettes in CI) so source-or-silence — the central claim — is actually defended on every PR.

Complete is not "more features." It is the discipline held all the way down to one real user's real trip. The sequenced path that gets us there is [`strategy/path-to-complete.md`](strategy/path-to-complete.md).

## The pillars we own

Five pillars, each a position no engagement-funded incumbent can occupy. For each: why it's ours, and the proof a user can see.

**1. Honest uncertainty as one property on every fact.** The entire field shows undated, unsourced data as current truth; nobody unifies freshness × authority × corroboration into one honestly-hedged confidence at the *app* level. We already ship a taste-blind, computed-on-read confidence and three honesty UI primitives — but the corroboration axis is a constant `1` (a single tier-1 source and a multiply-corroborated fact are indistinguishable). Owning this means *actually* implementing independence-checked corroboration (CDP-01: walk to distinct **origin** nodes, fuse weakest-link, never count echoing feeds, never re-ingest our own output).
> *Proof:* a confidence chip that decomposes on tap into authority + freshness + corroboration; a fact confirmed by two **independent** origins reads "confirmed by 2 independent sources" while two feeds sharing one origin honestly read "counts as 1"; and four visibly distinct silence states (no-data / no-hazard / not-fetched / stale-degraded) mean an honest app in a quiet region never reads as broken or as falsely green. *(If the CDP-01 spike finds origin metadata unrecoverable, the "2 independent sources" line honestly degrades to "single authoritative source / counts as 1" — the **two-axis decomposition and the four silence states hold unconditionally**, so this pillar's proof never depends on the spike's outcome.)*

**2. Un-paywalled, un-staled live safety truth.** Incumbents paywall freshness because live API calls cost money — which is exactly why this space is open, and exactly why it must be a deliberate, cost-controlled *position*, not an unpriced wish. A utility that never gates safety/freshness behind a tier and never persists a JIT overlay is a trust position subscription incumbents structurally cannot occupy. This is also our biggest operational liability today: the public `/plan` endpoint fans out to live APIs + LLM with no rate-limiting, no metrics, and an unmeasured cost spike (R5).
> *Proof:* every live overlay (weather, streamflow, AQI, fire, permits) renders with its source and fetched-at time, never gated behind a tier, and visibly degrades past its per-data-type freshness window; the user gets the truth for free, and we keep it sustainable through JIT caching + tuned refresh windows + region pilots + rate-limiting.

**3. Private, outcome-learned personal intelligence (capability ≠ preference).** Capability sensors (Garmin/Oura/WHOOP/COROS) all drift into telling you what you *should* do; taste models (Letterboxd/Beli) learn preference but publicly and with no physiological reality-check. The fusion — pairwise-learned **private** preference + watch readiness used **only** as a non-ranking capability floor — is essentially unowned outdoors. We've built the substrate (Belief nodes tagged capability/preference, structural on-device forcing of the private overlay, the conflation/survivorship model) — but it runs on mock episodes for one seeded user because the intake is empty. This is the largest remaining value lever *and* the largest current gap.
> *Proof:* the user logs a trip and watches it privately sharpen the next answer ("your last 8 logged trips tightened these"); history import pre-warms taste from day one; readiness shows as a hedged capability band that tightens the effort floor but never reorders by desirability; and a trip the user keeps returning to is treated as history, never silently relabeled as preference — the reward for logging is a better answer, never a streak.

**4. Cockpit-grade calm: the tool scores, the human decides.** The most expensive documented failure across every high-stakes domain is not bad data but *burying the critical fact in a wall of true-but-trivial ones* (Air Canada 759; clinicians override 49–96% of alerts). "Cockpit-grade" is most often mis-read as *more* information; every domain proves the opposite. Discipline is **refusal** — refuse to fuse the two axes, refuse to let the verdict decide, refuse to interrupt without an action, refuse to count echoing sources. This is our anti-engagement spine made productive: a finite surface, a single binding constraint, an always-overridable advisory.
> *Proof:* one legible verdict (go / marginal / no-go) naming a single binding constraint as a tap-through live source, always overridable; non-actionable facts collapse into a muted tray; a safety-critical overlay always takes the top slot; notifications fire only with a cause + consequence + time + a corrective verb; and the whole answer is readable in seconds so the user can close the app — the success state is the user *leaving*, not lingering.

**5. Provenance-graph as the un-copyable foundation.** The four convergent moves are literally a graph-traversal architecture: walk lineage to distinct origins so corroboration is real; propagate staleness and grants along those same edges; keep the slow substrate immutable while ephemera stay JIT. A subscription incumbent on a relational conditions table physically cannot do this. We've built the rigorous core (query-layer access control CI-proven with falsifiability, born-severed commons fork, provenance edges, persona/conclusion conflation) — but the substrate has a landmine (a slug-collision *can* fuse two distinct *same-source* trails whose names slugify to an identical ≤40-char string — long names are already sha1-suffix-guarded, so the exposure is narrow but live; *whether it has actually fired in the loaded corpus is unknown until the Phase-A audit runs against re-runnable ingest*, but if it has, a wrong merge attaches a live safety overlay to the wrong real-world place) and asymmetric guards (the write path is structurally guarded; three owned reads are convention-only). The transitive-staleness and grant-on-edge half of this moat holds regardless of the corroboration spike's outcome; the independence-checked-corroboration half is the part the spike (Pillar 1) governs.
> *Proof:* a shared trip shows "Shared by Alex (derived recommendation)" with zero path back to Alex's private substrate; a revoked grant takes effect on the grantee's next view; a changed source flags every conclusion that depended on it; and the conflation pipeline never silently fuses two real trails — every merge is additive, reversible, threshold-and-degree-guarded, and the user can see each source's raw claim with disagreement shown as a range, never a fabricated average.

## Principles — the discipline that makes the pillars trustworthy

The six product invariants are not preferences; the cross-domain review reframes them as **discovered law**. They sit alongside the four convergent moves and the supporting corroboration/screening rules. Each is one line; full provenance lives in the two reviews.

**The six invariants (discovered law):**

1. **Source-or-silence.** Every user-facing fact is backed by a live source + timestamp; unverifiable is *flagged*, never fabricated — absence of a fresh source must render as absence of a claim, loudly. *(Invariant 1; CDP-02)*
2. **Confidence is one property** (freshness × authority × corroboration) that sets a floor, sets the presentation (hedged when low), and acts as a safety flag — and **never** penalizes desirability ranking. The diagonal-collapse (fusing the two axes into one habitual score) is the named anti-pattern. *(Invariant 2; convergent move 1; CDP-07)*
3. **Slow corpus + JIT overlays.** The graph holds slow/structural data only; fast/ephemeral data (weather, streamflow, AQI, permits, readiness) is fetched just-in-time and never persisted as nodes to stale — per-data-type freshness windows, stale-while-revalidate, then loud silence. *(Invariant 3; CDP-08)*
4. **Access control at the query/data layer, never in the agent** — the single Check-style choke-point; every traversal is permission-scoped to the viewer; honor it from the first schema. *(Invariant 4; CDP-12)*
5. **Private-by-default, share-by-exception.** Share the derived *conclusion*, never the raw substrate; strip residual metadata before any emit (Strava's privacy-zone leak is the cautionary tale); enforced in Cypher, never in UI redaction. *(Invariant 5; CDP-12)*
6. **Provenance + confidence + timestamp on every user-belief.** An inference never poses as a stated fact; `capability ≠ preference` (the watch is a strong *named* source for capability, a weak/anonymous one for preference); the role tag is generated at the fetch boundary, not by remember-to-log diligence. *(Invariant 7; CDP-03)*

> *(The project's rules also include #6 anti-engagement and #8–10 commons/no-training/secrets; #6 is captured under Principle 12 below, the others under Non-goals.)*

**The four convergent moves (thirteen domains, uncoordinated):**

7. **The tool scores, the human decides.** One auditable advisory verdict naming a single binding constraint; the verdict is never an oracle ("the FRAT is not designed to make the go/no-go decision for you"); always overridable, and an override is recorded as preference signal. *(Convergent move 2; CDP-04)*
8. **Safety is a perishable state, re-established continuously** — not a one-time pre-trip check. Saved trips carry a live conditions-watch with pre-set triggers and a forward-framed recall ("best call from here"), never a silent auto-cancel and never a stale release served as fresh. *(Convergent move 3; CDP-11)*
9. **Corroboration is the engine, but independence is the governor** — count distinct **origin** nodes, not feeds, and never re-ingest our own output as external evidence (Curveball/Iraq circular reporting; Wikipedia citogenesis). This is the one we currently get wrong, so it is the highest-leverage fix *and* the moat itself. *(Convergent move 4; CDP-01)*

**The supporting fusion/screening rules:**

10. **Weakest-link, not average.** Fused confidence is `MIN` across the provenance chain, and a single critical hazard caps the safety verdict regardless of how good everything else is; never average a disagreement into a comfortable middle. *(CDP-06)*
11. **Non-compensatory screen, then compensatory rank.** Hard safety/capability constraints screen the candidate set *before* taste/novelty/party-fit rank the survivors; a beautiful-but-unsafe option can never be bought back, and ruled-out trips show their binding cutoff with its source. *(CDP-10)*
12. **Anti-engagement is also pro-accuracy.** Gamified logging poisons the very taste signal a recommender needs; no streaks, leaderboards, infinite scroll, manufactured anxiety, or readiness-as-a-score. The only legitimate logging incentive is a better answer next time; success is the user getting the answer and closing the app. *(Invariant 6)*

## Non-goals — the refusals

The discipline *is* the refusals. These are load-bearing, not throat-clearing.

- **No streaks, consecutive-day counters, or any return-or-lose mechanic.** Accumulated context is a durable, lossless private asset — the inverse of Duolingo's flame, not a thing that dies at midnight.
- **No leaderboards, crowns, kudos, badges, or approval metrics.** They corrupt the taste signal (Strava Local Legends incentivized cheating at scale and physically dangerous riding) and break calm.
- **No infinite scroll and no for-you, dwell-optimized feed.** The curated drop is finite and ends; finiteness is driven by what's *verifiable*, never padded to hit a quota.
- **No readiness surfaced as a score, ring, target, or streak.** A capability sensor turned into a daily goal is a documented preference-distorting stressor; readiness is a hedged, non-ranking, capability-only effort floor that degrades-and-discloses.
- **No anthropomorphized, guilt, or "we'll stop messaging you" notifications.** Notify only on a live, source-backed, time-sensitive reason with a stated corrective verb; a flood of buzzes during volatile weather collapses into one consolidated push.
- **No fabricated affect or confident black-box scores posing as measured truth** (the Garmin/Oura/WHOOP categorical-verdict failure). An inference always reads hedged; a learned preference is never persisted as a stated fact.
- **No paywalling of safety or freshness behind a tier, and no hardware lock-in.** Anonymous browsing of the world + live conditions is a real, un-gated product; auth gates only the personal overlay.
- **No social network, follow graph, or public profile.** Sharing is a utility action — a revocable, time-boxed, no-re-share grant of a derived conclusion — never a feed or a status object.
- **No model training and no selling of personal data.** Pure orchestration; the commons fork is born-severed and de-identified, aggregated only above the k-anonymity floor.
- **No model that biases toward already-popular trails** (the Matthew-effect / graph-recommender failure). We keep an explicit novelty term and rank obscure-but-promising candidates on slow-corpus structural facts rather than suppressing them.
- **No false completeness.** The feed never implies exhaustiveness — a non-zero "none of these / we can't confidently match yet" state is always legitimate (SAR's rest-of-world residual mass).

## The north-star moment of use

Saturday, 6:40am. Josh opens the app from his kitchen with the dog at his feet. The feed shows three verified trips near him; the top card reads **"Marginal — go if you start before noon."** One tap expands the *why*: the binding constraint is afternoon thunderstorms (60%, per NWS, issued 2 hours ago, named and timestamped), and the streamflow at the one creek ford reads green — "confirmed by 2 independent sources" — while the trail's distance is quietly marked as un-reverified structural data, not dressed up as live truth. A fourth trip he'd normally consider isn't ranked at all; it sits in a "Ruled out (1)" tray that names exactly why — a fire closure, with its NPS source.

Nothing nags him, nothing streaks, nothing scrolls forever. He reads the whole thing in under a minute, trusts it because he can see its work, picks the early start, closes the app, and drives to the trailhead. That evening he taps the trip he took, logs "loved it, finished strong," and the app — privately, on his device — files that as one more outcome that will tighten next Saturday's answer. No leaderboard, no audience, no manufactured reason to come back: just a calm utility that was right when it mattered and got quietly smarter for having helped.

## How we'll know it's working (non-engagement success)

Success is measured in **honest decisions, not engagement.**

1. **Trip-decision quality (the primary metric).** Of the trips a user took on our verdict, what fraction did they later rate good — and, inversely, did any bad-outcome trip carry a MARGINAL/NO-GO verdict with the correct binding constraint named (a *calibration* test, not a hit count).
2. **Calm.** The user reads the answer and closes the app. We explicitly do **not** track DAU, session length, or scroll depth — and we would treat *rising* session time as a regression.
3. **Epistemic integrity, gated automatically.** Confidence is *calibrated* (a 0.8 is true ~80% of the time, checked by a Brier/reliability hook in Epic 009, not merely self-consistent); a fact's confidence rises only when origins are genuinely **independent** (CDP-01's falsification test); a safety-critical overlay takes the top visual slot 100% of the time and the median actionable flags-per-trip is ≤1 (no critical fact buried); every verdict is re-derivable from its shown work; and source-or-silence is defended on every PR by golden trips + cassettes in CI — which today has no automated regression gate against the real engine.
4. **Substrate trust.** Zero silently-merged trails (the slug-collision audit passes), zero cross-owner leaks (already CI-proven), and provenance + confidence + timestamp present on 100% of user-facing facts.
5. **Real, not seeded.** The value prop is demonstrable on **real** data, not mock episodes. Two named proof points, not one all-or-nothing finale: *the loop is real* the moment **one** real user signs in, logs a trip, and gets an answer from their **own** data (the first go/no-go on the long runway); *the product is provably real* the moment a **second** real household member gets an honestly-personalized answer from their own logged history and can share a derived conclusion without leaking the substrate.

**The single sentence:** *we are winning when a person trusts our answer enough to act on it, the answer was right, and they spent less time in the app because of it.*
