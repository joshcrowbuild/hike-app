One card = your plan and its conditions. The trick you called out: align conditions to the frame’s when, and split what can be forecast from what can only be “right now.”

Today is Monday. Your frame says “Weekend morning.” So weather forecasts to Saturday — not today. AQI and streamflow can’t be forecast that far, so they show as “right now,” honestly flagged that they may change by Saturday. This replaces the separate ribbon (Q4) — the area conditions now live inside this card.

The sentence treatment, fixed

You’re right — the dotted underline reads as a spellcheck error. Two cleaner ways to signal “editable,” if we go sentence over card (or blend them).

a — solid hairline underline (a link, not an error)
Weekend mornings, from Front Royal, going solo at a moderate pace.
A steady terracotta underline reads as “link,” never “misspelled.” Warmest, most editorial.
b — soft inline chips
Weekend mornings from Front Royal , solo moderate
Each value is a bordered chip with a caret — unmistakably a control. Loses some prose warmth, gains clarity (this is basically the Filter Bar, sentence-shaped).
