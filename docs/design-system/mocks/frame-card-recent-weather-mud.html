The mud read is a hedged inference — amber, dashed, tagged “inferred,” with its evidence (“1.2in in 48h”) and source (NWS obs). It says may, never “is.” It’s a heads-up, not a hazard, so it’s amber, not terracotta.

Honesty guardrails for mud: rain is a proxy, not proof (soil, sun, elevation, drainage all matter), so it stays hedged and disclosed. Worth an eval later — does the precip→mud rule actually hold on our trails? That, plus the recent-obs fetch, is why this reads as its own tight feature rather than a free add-on. Static prototype.
