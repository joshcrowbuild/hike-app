The states — everything past the happy path

For a source-or-silence app the states are the product. Color encodes actionability, not confidence: gray = we don't know (calm), amber = heads-up (you're fine), brick = you're blocked (a real barrier). Silence = clear. Red is retired — it only appears in the "before" panels to show what we're fixing.
